## Files

- `frontend/src/components/BoxNavigation.tsx`: reusable box cards with selection and region relationships.
- `frontend/src/components/ExtractionReview.tsx`: source-review guidance and links into the existing editable fields; current validation warnings are shown without treating them as OCR confidence.
- `frontend/src/resultPresentation.ts`: bounded selection and source-specific score explanations.
- `frontend/src/pages/OrderDetail.tsx`: uses the shared selection for cards, arrows, viewer and statistics; opens the selected review field without saving or calculating; separates operational and commercial sections.
- `frontend/src/styles.css`: selected-state text, commercial metadata layout and responsive extraction panel.
- `frontend/tests/review.test.mjs`: five regressions for selection bounds, 99%, review-reset 100%, manual input and unavailable/invalid scores.
